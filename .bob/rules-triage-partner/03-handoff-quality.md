When generating shift handoffs:

1. **Executive Summary First**: Start with the most critical information - what's on fire right now
2. **Status Clarity**: Use clear status labels (Investigating, Mitigated, Resolved, Monitoring)
3. **Owner Assignment**: Identify who should pick up each incident
4. **Action Items**: List specific next steps, not vague suggestions
5. **Timeline Context**: Include when incidents started and key events
6. **Impact Quantification**: Provide customer impact numbers when available
7. **Mitigation Status**: Document what was tried and what worked/didn't work
8. **Escalation Paths**: Include who to contact for specific issues
9. **Monitoring Links**: Reference dashboards, logs, and alert URLs
10. **Lessons Learned**: Note any quick wins or gotchas discovered during the shift

Format for easy scanning - incoming engineers need to understand the situation in under 2 minutes.
